Kids Box 26.4.12

- Added Check & play to every unit, with 72 new challenges across three activities.
- Listen & place: follow spoken instructions to move original book pictures on, under or next to furniture. Touch dragging, tap-to-place and keyboard controls are supported; cancelled and outside drops do not count as wrong answers.
- Build a sentence: tap or drag spoken word/phrase cards into an answer, then check it. Undo, remove cards and try again; hear the complete sentence after solving it.
- Look & fix: judge a description against the original book picture. When it is wrong, choose the corrected sentence to complete the challenge.
- Instructions, picture choices, word cards and correction options have offline audio. Speaker buttons never submit answers. Every unit has two challenges of each form, with feedback, celebration and a first-try result for the current round.
- Added 116 British practice clips and reused existing recordings. All 222 spoken texts for the new challenges are included; existing Cambridge, alphabet and original book audio are unchanged.
- Activity navigation now uses a responsive grid to keep all eight entries visible. Existing learning records, settings and added resources are preserved when updating.

To update on Android, open Parent guide → App updates. Confirm installation after the download finishes.
