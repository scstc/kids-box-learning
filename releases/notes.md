Kids Box 26.4.13

- Add local learner profiles. Enter a name or choose a saved learner to continue their own words, practice bag, book activities, challenges and last reading page. The first learner inherits existing progress; later learners start fresh. No account or internet connection is needed.
- My progress shows each learner’s completed book activities and grammar/play challenges. All 108 challenges now remember successful answers, with a Practised before label on revisits.
- Add Write to the fullscreen talking book, with Pen, Eraser and Undo. Use a stylus, finger or mouse; notes stay aligned to the original page and remain visible when returning to point reading.
- Handwriting saves automatically on this device, separately for every learner and page. Save feedback and Retry cover background pages too; unreadable notes are protected from accidental overwriting.
- Clear learning records now affects only the current learner. Handwritten notes, other learners, settings and added pictures/audio are kept. Existing Cambridge, alphabet, phonics and book media are unchanged.

Local records do not sync between devices. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes local progress and notes.

To update on Android, open Parent guide → App updates. Confirm installation after the download finishes.
